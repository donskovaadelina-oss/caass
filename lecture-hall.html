CAASS Lecture Hall

Аккредитованная 5-недельная программа создания научного контента

CAASS Lecture Hall is an accredited selective 5-week programme of academically rigorous scholarly content creation. Our lecturers plan out and design their own 4-lecture mini-courses on a topic of their interest within the realm of social science and humanities. It is a truly unique and effective way to enhance their portfolios, share about the theories and phenomena they find enticing and communicate with like-minded aspiring scholars across Central Asia. By the end of the programme, the lecturers and regular listeners receive certificates accredited by UNESCO Federation of Clubs in Kazakhstan.
